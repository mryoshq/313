How timing works

Study periods play your current mix; breaks are silent. Fade-out lowers the volume during the final seconds of each study period or sleep timer. Pause also pauses the countdown. Without automatic continuation, each new phase waits for you to resume. Reset stops playback. There is no break after the final round.

What am I seeing?

Frequency is the number of cycles per second, measured in hertz (Hz). Amplitude is the size of a wave: increasing a row's level makes its curve larger and its tone louder. At the default visual speed, the animation runs 20 times slower so you can see its shape. Visual settings do not change the sound. The animation freezes when paused.
